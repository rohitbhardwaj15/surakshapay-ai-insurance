1️⃣ Problem & Why It Matters

Delivery partners in hyperlocal platforms (Zepto/Blinkit-style ecosystems) operate on weekly income cycles and are highly exposed to environmental disruptions:

Heavy rainfall
AQI spikes
Heatwaves
Curfew / flood conditions

When disruptions occur:

Working hours drop instantly
Orders decline
Incentives disappear

👉 Result: 20–30% weekly income loss

Traditional insurance systems fail due to:

Slow claim processing
Manual workflows
High operational overhead
2️⃣ Solution Overview

SurakshaPay is a parametric, AI-powered income protection system that automates the entire lifecycle:

Rider onboarding (city, platform, income)
AI risk scoring (0–1)
Dynamic premium calculation
Policy activation with capped coverage
Real-time trigger detection
Automated claim processing
Instant payout visibility
Fraud detection & review logic
